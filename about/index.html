About Us

Our Story

In 2019, Sarah Cotrupi, the visionary behind The Porch, fell in love with a charming Victorian building. Together, with her husband Mark and sister Christine, they transformed the historic gem into a vibrant home decor, antique, and gift shop.

Sarah’s journey from her successful retail ventures in Nebraska City, NE with The Keeping Room, to The Porch here in Sandwich NH reflects her dedication and passion for creating welcoming spaces. At The Porch, Sarah, Mark, and Chris bring this passion and retail experience to curate a diverse array of unique gifts, ensuring every visitor enjoys a delightful and fulfilling shopping experience.

Visit The Porch today and discover the magic of a thoughtfully curated selection of home decor and antiques, perfect for any occasion!
